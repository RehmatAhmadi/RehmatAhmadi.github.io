Ahead

Bill reminders for iPhone

Ahead finds the bills that arrive in your email, reads the amount and due date, keeps a copy of the original PDF on your iPhone, and reminds you every day from a few days before the due date until you mark the bill paid.

How it uses your email

When you connect a Gmail account, Ahead asks Google for read-only access. It looks for bill emails, reads them on your iPhone, and saves the bill details and PDF on the device. It never sends, deletes or changes your email, and nothing is sent to our servers — Ahead doesn't have any.
