Privacy policy

Ahead for iPhone · Effective 11 October 2026

Ahead is developed by Rehmat in Melbourne, Australia. This policy explains what the app accesses and what happens to it.

Summary

  • Everything Ahead reads stays on your iPhone.
  • Ahead has no servers and no accounts. We never receive, store, sell or share your data.
  • Email access is read-only and you can remove it at any time.

Email data (Google user data)

If you connect a Gmail account, Ahead requests the gmail.readonly permission. It uses it only to:

  • search your mailbox for bill emails (for example from energy, water, internet, toll and registration providers);
  • read those emails and their PDF attachments on your device, to extract the provider, amount, due date and payment details;
  • save a copy of the bill and its PDF in the app on your iPhone, and match payment-confirmation emails to bills.

Ahead never sends, deletes, labels or changes your email. Emails that aren't bills are skipped and not stored.

Your Google sign-in token is stored in the iPhone Keychain. It is used only to connect your device directly to Google's Gmail service.

Ahead's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not transferred to anyone, is not used for advertising, is not used to train artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models, and is not read by any person.

Other data you add

PDFs, screenshots or text you add yourself are processed and stored the same way: on your iPhone only.

Notifications

Bill reminders are scheduled locally on your iPhone by iOS. No reminder data leaves your device.

Backups

If you use iCloud or computer backups, your iPhone's backup may include Ahead's data. Those backups are handled by Apple under your own settings.

Removing your data

  • Disconnect an email account in Ahead → Settings → Email. This removes the sign-in token from your iPhone.
  • Revoke Ahead's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.
  • Deleting a bill removes it and its PDF. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your iPhone.

Children

Ahead is not directed at children under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
