What Ahead does

Ahead helps you keep track of bills by reading bill emails and documents you choose, and by reminding you of due dates. It is a personal organisation tool.

Ahead does not make payments

Ahead never pays a bill or moves money. Marking a bill as paid only records it in the app. You are responsible for paying your bills on time.

Check the details

Amounts, due dates and payment details are read automatically and can be wrong or incomplete. Always check them against the original bill before paying. Ahead doesn't verify that a payment link or email is genuine — confirm you are on your provider's real website before entering card details.

Reminders

Reminders depend on iOS notifications, your notification settings, Focus modes and Background App Refresh. They may be delayed or not delivered, and you shouldn't rely on them as your only record of a bill.
